FLIPNOVA (Windows)
1) Unzip this folder anywhere.
2) Double-click FLIPNOVA.exe.
F11 = fullscreen. Your progress is saved on this computer.
If Windows SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info" then "Run anyway" (the game is not signed with a paid certificate).

فك الضغط عن الفولدر، ودوس دبل كليك على FLIPNOVA.exe.  F11 = شاشة كاملة.
لو ويندوز قال "Windows protected your PC": دوس "More info" وبعدين "Run anyway".
