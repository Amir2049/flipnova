# Evaluation: AFC (mobile 11-a-side football) - 2026-11-26

## 1. Verdict: PIVOT (not KILL)
The game is real and playable, but head-on against EA FC Mobile, eFootball and DLS on looks and licences it cannot
win; positioned as **the fair, offline, Arabic football game for everyday phones** it has a gap the leaders leave open.

## 2. Core loop
Pick a club -> play a 6-10 minute 11-a-side match with touch controls (pass, shoot, skill gestures) -> win, earn
progress in career / tournament / challenges -> fail state: lose the match, drop in the table.

Notes for an existing game: **distance to a shippable beta: ~2-4 weeks** (store listing, privacy policy, first-run
tutorial, crash/ANR check on 3-4 real phones, ads SDK). **Reusable parts:** the whole engine and its gameplay work,
touch controls, career, UI kit - all carry into the pivot. Sunk cost is not a reason to continue; the gap below is.

## 5. Gaps to exploit
1. **Fair play**: no pay-to-win - ratings come from your club, not your wallet (FC Mobile's top complaint).
2. **Offline and light**: plays fully offline, 36 MB APK, runs on mid phones (FC/eFootball need GBs and a server).
3. **Arabic and Egyptian identity**: Arabic UI and commentary, Egyptian clubs/streets, the Hara street mode - none
   of the leaders speaks to this audience directly. Football League 2026 shows a small studio can reach 12M/month
   with an offline, light game.

## 6. Pivot or sharpen
**One change:** stop chasing FC on graphics; ship an Arabic-first, offline, fair football game - Arabic UI and
names, an Egyptian league/street career, short matches, rewarded ads only - and get it on Google Play as an open
beta within a month. Re-scored: Virality 3 (local identity clips), Competition gap 3 (a niche the leaders ignore),
Trend timing 3, others as above -> **22/35**, still PIVOT territory but with a clear target; GO once the beta's
numbers clear the kill switch.

## 7. Kill switch (hand-off to tracking-game-metrics)
Open beta, first 1,000 organic installs:
- D1 retention < 25 % or D7 < 6 % after two update cycles -> stop the 11-a-side push, keep Hara street only.
- Crash/ANR rate > 2 % on Play Console -> fix before any growth.
- Average session < 6 min (shorter than one match) -> the match length or controls are wrong.
These thresholds are typical sports-genre benchmarks, not live-sourced: re-check them with tracking-game-metrics.
