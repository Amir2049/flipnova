# Flippo Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 November 2026. Draft: review before publishing._

Flippo (formerly FLIPNOVA) is a game made by one independent developer ("we"). This page explains what the game stores and sends.

## What stays on your device
Your progress (coins, characters, stages, settings) is saved on your device only. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. You can copy a progress code yourself from Settings.

## What is sent to our server
Only when you play online features (the world leaderboard, friends board, gift codes):
- the **player name** you choose,
- your **scores and run details** (distance, stage, character, and the taps of the run so it can be checked for cheating),
- a **random device id** created by the game (not your phone's hardware id).

This data is stored with our database provider (Supabase) to show the leaderboards. Names and scores are public on the leaderboards.

## What we do not do
- No ads and no ad networks.
- No analytics or tracking SDKs.
- No in-app purchases.
- We do not collect your email, location, contacts, photos or phone number.
- We do not sell or share your data.

## Children
The game does not ask for personal information. A player name can be anything; please don't use your real full name.

## Deleting your data
Data is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). To remove your name and scores from the leaderboards, contact us at **amirabd204@gmail.com** with your player name.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
