# Prototype: PULSE

- **Hypothesis:** the one-tap flip feels flat because FLIPNOVA asks for a tap only every ~2.6 s (0.38 taps/s, 40% of a run
  needs no input). If EVERY hazard needs a tap, at a steady ~1 tap/s that speeds up, with score = seconds survived and an
  instant retry, the flip itself becomes fun.
- **Run:** open `index.html` in a phone browser in landscape. Tap anywhere to flip. After a death, tap to go again; the
  three TEMPO buttons set the starting rhythm (0.7 / 1 / 1.3 taps per second).
- **Keep if:** Amir plays "one more" run without being asked, and the tap feels like a beat, not a chore.
- **Kill if:** still boring at tempo 1 and 1.3, or it only feels good with art and content on top.
- **Status:** concluded (17 Nov 2026). Throwaway: never imported by the game.
- **Findings:** Amir: "better, but not enough". Decision: no separate mode; its lesson (a tap on every beat, faster retry) goes into Endless rules v8 (BEATs in openings and finales, alternating runs, shorter calms, 0.45 s death-to-results, 0.3 s tap-to-retry).
